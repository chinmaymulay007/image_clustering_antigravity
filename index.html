App Instructions

1. Click "Select Folder" and pick a directory containing your photos.
2. ClusterAI (using local AI) will group similar images into clusters automatically.
3. This will take time, but clusters update in real-time as photos are analysed.
4. Review the groups and exclude any photos you don't wish to use.
5. Clusters improve dynamically as more images are processed.
6. You need exactly 6 clusters with 16 photos each for Passfaces setup.
7. Lock the clusters you want, then proceed to upload to Passfaces.
8. All AI processing happens 100% locally on your device. Your photos are not sent to any server.
9. Only your final selections (6x16) are uploaded to the Passfaces app in compressed format.
